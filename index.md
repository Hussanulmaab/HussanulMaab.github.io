Hi traveller. Welcome to my small town and feel free to roam around. I'm a master's student in the department of Electronics and Information Convergence at
**Kyung Hee University** in South Korea in my last semester. Before Korea I did my bachelor's in
Computer and Information Sciences at the Pakistan Institute of Engineering
and Applied Sciences in Islamabad. I am to graduate from masters and Communications
and Quantum Information Lab (CQILAB) in February 2027
and prospectively join Quantum Computing Lab as a post Masters Research Fellow under professor Jeong San Kim from March
2027 to August 2027 before commencing my PhD program in Fall 2027. During the 2nd year of my Computer Science
degree I attended a Lecture on Quantum Computation by Pakistani Physicist Pervez Hoodbhoy,
after which I decided I will be studying theoretical quantum physics. I wanted to pursue
Double Major in Physics alongside my CS degree but my university policies were not supportive
of that so I decided to study myself.

In my undergrad I worked on **quantum key distribution** (QKD). In my Masters,
I switched to **quantum error correction** (QEC) because of its connections
to Pure Maths, Theoretical Physics, Applied Physics, Theoretical Computer Sciences.
This mixture of everything was what pulled me towards QEC. In my masters, I build tensor network
decoders for the tetrahedral 3D color codes. At small
distances it reaches roughly half the logical error rate of the best decoder
published so far. The code is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Hussanulmaab/TNDecoder3DColor), and a manuscript
is in preparation for _Physical Review Research_. Earlier, for my
undergraduate thesis, I worked on the postprocessing side of quantum key
distribution, reconciling information and extracting randomness, which became
my first paper in _Quantum Information Processing_. In 2026 I was accepted at
the AlgoLab summer school on fault tolerant quantum computing in Sherbrooke,
and at the European Tensor Networks School at the Isaac Newton Institute at the
University of Cambridge to present my work.

Alongside the physics I occasionally study pure maths for fun, and I also tutor
mathematics and quantum computing to students around the world at Preply and Wiingy tutoring platforms.
During my free time (When I am not contemplating), I like to play Classical music, or
I am either at the gym (started calisthenics a few weeks ago), running, hiking, enjoying nature,
meeting new people, hanging out with friends, teaching, exploring, discussing philosophy, ideas, maths and physics,
or something random depending upon the day. I am also a big fan of classical music and my favourite composer
is Ludovico Einaudi and one of my dreams is to see him play Experience at the Royal albert Hall.
Feel free to reach my anywhere anytime to discuss anything. I love people who are **quiet interesting**.
